01 / Detection

Detect

Incoming prompt payloads are vector-transformed via sublinear n-gram tokenization and scored against a calibrated linear decision boundary in under 3 milliseconds on CPU.

  • Sublinear term-frequency weights eliminate token stuffing evasion
  • Optimal 0.4909 decision threshold calibrated against F1 optimum
  • Zero GPU overhead, operating seamlessly as sidecar microservice
02 / Quarantine

Respond

Warden maintains a hard, non-negotiable security boundary. Verified benign queries pass directly to your LLM; adversarial vectors hit an immediate zero-token quarantine.

  • Deterministic isolation prevents adversarial payloads from reaching LLM
  • Preserves 100% of downstream tokens and prevents prompt injection leaks
  • Safe prompts answered with live multi-model Gemini 2.5 synthesis
